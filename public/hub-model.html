JCT Performance Hub
A related, facility-light racquet-sport fitness & performance business — module by module
Not a tennis club, not a generic gym — the JCT Performance Hub: a racquet-sport fitness & performance facility. People train here (fitness classes, simulators, rally lanes, smart walls, physio) and play elsewhere — or come back here between programs, since the Hub manages their training regardless of where they play. Build the facility one module at a time: each module is a space (owned/operated, or rented out to an operator) with its own footprint and economics. The totals below roll up into a rough live picture of the whole model — swap estimates for real numbers as they firm up.
