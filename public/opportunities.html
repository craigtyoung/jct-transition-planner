Opportunities
Target clubs and deal conversations — every path to JCT's next home, tracked in one pipeline
JCT's real asset is the academy (~72% of revenue), the members and waitlist, and the coaching expertise — not the building. This board tracks the clubs, partners, and land we could bring that to. Each card is one opportunity: set the category (real estate listing, private school, athletic club, or other) and deal type, move its status along the pipeline, and give it a priority number to pin your top picks to the top. Log who owns the relationship and the next action so nothing goes cold.

Two build-partner pitches, not one: schools get the "we build on your unused land, nominal lease, you get exclusive access" pitch. Golf & country clubs get a different one — a seasonal dome over their existing outdoor courts for the off-season, since golf (spring–fall) and JCT's dome season (fall–spring, academy-only in summer) are complementary, not competing. See the Communications page for both letter templates.
